Our Story

More Than Just Agents

FADARA Football Agency was founded by three passionate young entrepreneurs in Sierra Leone with a shared vision: to identify, nurture, and promote local football talent. Recognizing the need for professional guidance in the football industry, FADARA was created to bridge the gap between talented players and opportunities at both national and international levels. Since its inception, FADARA has been committed to providing mentorship, career management, and pathways for footballers to achieve their dreams.


With offices in Sierra Leone, Liberia, and Guinea, we bridge the gap between young talent and top-tier clubs globally. Our philosophy is transparency, integrity, and relentless ambition.

our vision

To become Sierra Leone’s most trusted and internationally recognized football agency, empowering young talents to rise from grassroots to global stages through professional development, mentorship, and world-class representation.

Our mission at FADARA Football Agency is to:

  • Discover, develop, and promote talented footballers across Sierra Leone and beyond
  • Provide professional guidance, mentorship, and career management that helps players reach their full potential.
  • Support youth football programs and build a pathway that turns passion into professional success.

What We Do At FADARA Agency

  • Talent Scouting – Discovering talented football players across Sierra Leone
  • Player Development – Providing training, coaching, and mentorship to enhance skills and performance.
  • Career Management – Guiding players on contracts, transfers, and professional growth.
  • Football Programs & Community Engagement – Supporting youth football initiatives and creating platforms for young players to showcase their talent.
